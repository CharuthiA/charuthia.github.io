About

Engineering medical devices with rigor, clarity, and measurable intent.

I’m a UC Davis Biomedical Engineering graduate moving deeper into advanced medical device R&D, where biomechanics, robotics, verification strategy, and rapid prototyping converge into functional hardware.

My work is driven by a systems-thinking mindset: understand the clinical need, translate ambiguity into requirements, prototype quickly, and validate performance with structured evidence. I’m most energized when analytical engineering meets hands-on building—CAD, test fixtures, automation, failure analysis, and design iteration.

Looking ahead, I’m focused on biomechanics-informed product development for medical devices that can move from concept to reliable, testable systems. I care about engineering decisions that survive real constraints: manufacturability, usability, safety, and repeatable performance.
